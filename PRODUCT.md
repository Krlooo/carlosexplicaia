# Product

Carlos explica IA (@carlosexplicaia): a Spanish-language AI channel on Instagram and TikTok, run by Carlos, a programmer who trains people at ITECAM. This repo holds its public web pages, starting with the lead-magnet landing for the free guide "Tu agente personal".

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages (krlooo.github.io/carlosexplicaia). Email signup posts to a Cloudflare Worker at lista.krlo.dev (Ownlist fork, in ../captacion). Kept as is: the user did not ask for a stack change.

## Users
Spanish people (Spain) who arrive from a short reel on their phone, often after commenting AGENTE. Mostly non-technical, curious about AI, used to ChatGPT. One thumb, a few seconds of attention. (Inferred from the brief.)

## Product Purpose
Get the visitor to leave their email and confirm it, in exchange for the 12-page PDF guide to set up a personal AI agent at home in one afternoon. Secondary: they recognise the channel and follow it.

## Positioning
"IA sin humo": a programmer who shows a real agent he uses every day, not prompts or hype. Gap in the Spanish market: nobody explains agents with premium short animation and a real working setup.

## Operating Context
Opened from an Instagram DM or the bio link, inside the in-app browser on a phone. Desktop is rare.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Free tools only. Resend free plan: 100 emails a day.
- Double opt-in by email; consent checkbox is mandatory (RGPD). Privacy page at /privacidad/.
- No invented proof: no follower counts, testimonials or download numbers until they exist.

## Brand Commitments
- Pink lab palette: #eeb3c9 ground pink, #e8126b magenta (action), #2b0515 deep maroon (ink), #fff4f8 pink white, #ff7fb0 bubblegum.
- Type: Anton (display, condensed caps) and Inter Tight (text). JetBrains Mono exists in the reels but is not used on the web.
- Voice: European Spanish, plain, first person, short sentences, no hype words.

## Evidence on Hand
The guide itself (12 pages, rendered PDF with cover and page images), the reel "AGENTE", Carlos's real agent (Hermes on WhatsApp). No photographs of Carlos yet.

## Product Principles
- One action per page.
- Show the real thing (the guide's pages, a real chat with the agent) instead of describing it.
- Honest and small: say exactly what the visitor gets and what happens with their email.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable at 390 px, 16 px minimum text, visible focus, form usable by keyboard, contrast AA on pink grounds.
